Profile

Half of engineering is finding out what breaks first.

I am a mechanical engineering student at OU, on the Sooner Racing Formula SAE powertrain group, with two summers of manufacturing work behind me at a shop machining aerospace and defense parts.

The thread through everything here is the same: something has to actually work, in hardware, against a real constraint. A radiator that has to fit a chassis that was not designed around it. A bracket that passes inspection and still will not assemble. A printed part that has to survive a Texas parking lot. I like the part of the job where the drawing meets the material and one of them has to give.
